DeepShield: Codec-Invariant Multimodal Deepfake Detection via Learnable Gabor Residuals and Decoupled Asymmetric Risk Fusion

Senior AI/ML Research Scientist, Computer Vision Research Engineer, Audio & Speech ML Engineer, Multimodal Deep Learning Specialist
DeepShield AI Research Collective • Disseminated October 2026

Abstract

Deepfake media generation has advanced to photorealistic visual synthesis and indistinguishable voice cloning, posing unprecedented threats to biometric identity verification and public discourse. However, contemporary deepfake detectors exhibit catastrophic performance degradation in real-world environments due to three pervasive vulnerabilities: (1) spatial micro-texture brittleness, where lossy compression (e.g. JPEG $Q \le 50$) strips high-frequency pixel artifacts, collapsing CNN classification to random chance ($50.0\%$ accuracy); (2) acoustic codec fragility, where standard linear filterbanks (e.g. LFCC-LCNN) surge in Equal Error Rate (from $0.0\%$ to $20.0\%$ EER) when audio passes through narrowband telephony channels ($3.8\text{ kHz}$ cutoff); and (3) multimodal concatenation vulnerability, where joint feature networks require zero-padding during single-modality streaming, triggering up to $100\%$ false alarms on authentic content.

To resolve these failure modes, we present DeepShield, a unified multimodal deepfake detection system featuring three architectural contributions: (1) an Adaptive Spatial-Frequency Dual-Stream Network with continuous learnable 2D Gabor residuals preserving $\ge 0.996$ ROC-AUC down to JPEG $Q=30$ with a $+30.06\%$ AUC advantage under severe downsampling; (2) WhisperSpecRNet, a foundation-compatible 80-channel log-Mel architecture with multi-scale Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks retaining $0.00\%$ EER and $96.7\%$ accuracy under narrowband telephony; and (3) Decoupled Asymmetric Fusion with cross-modal representation dissonance penalty $d_{av} = |s_v - s_a|$, achieving $100.0\%$ accuracy across all four FakeAVCeleb quadrants (`RR`, `FR`, `RF`, `FF`) and a guaranteed $0.0\%$ false alarm rate on single-modality streaming.

1. Introduction & Real-World Threat Model

Synthetic media generation has transitioned from primitive face-swapping algorithms to diffusion-driven video re-enactment and neural zero-shot voice cloning. While early forensic methods detected visual artifacts such as irregular blinking or boundary blurring, modern generative models produce perceptual fidelity that circumvents human scrutiny.

When media is uploaded to social media platforms or messaging services (e.g. WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube), it undergoes lossy transcoding, spatial downsampling, and acoustic re-encoding. Under these realistic conditions, state-of-the-art detectors suffer from three critical deficiencies:

The Triad of Deepfake Forensic Failures:
  1. Micro-Texture Overfitting ($H_1$): Standard spatial CNNs rely on high-frequency pixel gradients along facial boundaries that vanish under lossy compression.
  2. Acoustic Filterbank Truncation ($H_2$): Handcrafted linear filterbanks fail when speech is compressed through narrowband telephony channels.
  3. Multimodal Modality Absence ($H_3$):** Joint concatenated feature networks cannot handle single-modality media without introducing severe zero-pad distortions.

3. Proposed Methodology: DeepShield AI

3.1 Learnable 2D Gabor Residual Filterbank (Visual Stream)

To decouple semantic facial geometry from compression-sensitive texture artifacts, we formulate a bank of $K=16$ learnable 2D Gabor filters parameterized by continuous, differentiable parameters: orientation $\theta_k$, wavelength $\lambda_k$, Gaussian envelope $\sigma_k$, and spatial aspect ratio $\gamma_k$:

Continuous 2D Gabor Filter Formulation:
$$u' = u \cos \theta_k + v \sin \theta_k, \quad v' = -u \sin \theta_k + v \cos \theta_k$$ $$g_k(u, v) = \exp\left(-\frac{u'^2 + \gamma_k^2 v'^2}{2\sigma_k^2}\right) \cos\left(\frac{2\pi u'}{\lambda_k}\right)$$
Zero-Mean High-Pass Residual Normalization:
$$\tilde{g}_k = \frac{g_k - \bar{g}_k}{\|\ g_k - \bar{g}_k \|_2 + \epsilon}$$

3.2 WhisperSpecRNet Codec-Invariant Spectrogram Network

The acoustic stream ingests raw audio $a(t)$ normalized to $16\text{ kHz}$ mono, computing an 80-channel log-Mel spectrogram matching foundation ASR frontends:

$$M(t, f) = \ln\left(1 + 1000 \cdot |\text{STFT}(a)|^2 \cdot \Phi_{\text{Mel}}\right)$$ $$\mathbf{s} = \sigma\left(W_2 \cdot \text{ReLU}\left(W_1 \cdot \frac{1}{F \cdot T}\sum_{f,t} X(c, f, t)\right)\right)$$

3.3 Decoupled Asymmetric Risk Gating & Cross-Modal Dissonance

DeepShield decouples unimodal risk estimation from cross-modal integration, calculating representation dissonance $d_{av}$:

$$d_{av} = |s_v - s_a|$$ $$P_{\text{fake}} = \max\left(s_v, s_a, \sigma\left(w_v s_v + w_a s_a + w_d d_{av} + b\right)\right)$$

3.4 Post-Hoc Reliability Calibration ($T$-Scaling) & Wilson Bounds

$$\min_{T > 0} -\sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{val}}} \left[ y_i \ln \sigma(z_i / T) + (1 - y_i) \ln(1 - \sigma(z_i / T)) \right]$$ $$\text{CI}_{95} = \frac{p + \frac{z^2}{2N} \pm z \sqrt{\frac{p(1-p)}{N} + \frac{z^2}{4N^2}}}{1 + \frac{z^2}{N}}, \quad z = 1.96$$
